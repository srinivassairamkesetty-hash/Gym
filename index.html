PUSH DAY
Chest · Shoulders · Triceps — Width makes your waist look slimmer before losing a single kg
CHESTSHOULDERSTRICEPS~50 MIN
EXERCISES — TAP VIDEO TO WATCH FORM
EX 1
Incline Dumbbell Press
Upper Chest · Front Delts
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
  • 1Set bench to 30–45° incline. Sit back with a dumbbell in each hand resting on thighs.
  • 2Kick the dumbbells up as you lie back, holding them at chest level, palms forward.
  • 3Press up and slightly inward until arms extended — don't fully lock elbows at top.
  • 4Lower slowly in 2–3 seconds feeling a deep chest stretch at the bottom.
  • 5Back slightly arched, feet flat, core engaged throughout every rep.
Trainer Tip: Start with 8–10kg. Feel it in your upper chest, not shoulders. If shoulders take over, lower the bench angle slightly.
EX 2
Lateral Raises
Side Delts — Creates Shoulder Width
3Sets
15Reps
45sRest
4–6kgWeight
  • 1Stand feet shoulder-width, dumbbells at sides, palms inward. Slight bend in elbows.
  • 2Raise both arms out to sides until parallel to floor — no higher than shoulder height.
  • 3At the top, tilt pinky slightly higher — like pouring water from a jug. Activates side delt fully.
  • 4Lower slowly in 2–3 seconds. Zero swinging or momentum allowed.
Trainer Tip: Wide shoulders = smaller-looking waist. Use very light weight — if traps burn more than shoulders, drop the weight immediately.
EX 3
Tricep Pushdown
Triceps — Horseshoe Definition
3Sets
15Reps
45sRest
  • 1Attach bar or rope to high pulley of cable machine.
  • 2Stand facing machine, slight forward lean. Elbows pinned tight to sides — they must not move.
  • 3Push down until arms fully extended. Squeeze triceps hard for 1 second at the bottom.
  • 4Allow bar to rise slowly in 2 seconds. Elbows stay locked to sides throughout.
Trainer Tip: Triceps = 2/3 of your arm size. This builds the horseshoe definition that makes arms look strong and toned.
🏃
Finish: 15 Min Incline Treadmill Walk
Speed 5.5–6 km/h · Incline 8–10% · Burns 2× more calories than flat walking. Your belly fat weapon.

02
PULL DAY
Back · Biceps — Strong back fixes posture & makes you look 4kg lighter instantly
BACKBICEPS~50 MIN
EXERCISES
EX 1
Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip)
Lats · Upper Back — The V-Taper Muscle
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
  • 1Sit at lat pulldown, thighs secured. Grip bar wider than shoulder-width, palms facing away.
  • 2Lean back slightly (15–20°), chest up and proud throughout.
  • 3Pull bar toward upper chest, LEADING WITH ELBOWS — think "elbows into back pockets."
  • 4Hold 1 second squeezing lats hard. Release slowly upward in 3 seconds.
Trainer Tip: Lats are your widest muscle — building them creates a V-shape that makes your belly look smaller from every angle.
EX 2
Seated Cable Row
Mid Back · Rhomboids · Rear Delts
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
  • 1Sit on cable row, feet on platform, knees slightly bent. Grab V-bar, sit tall, back straight.
  • 2Pull handle toward lower abdomen, elbows close to body throughout.
  • 3PINCH shoulder blades together hard for 1–2 seconds. This is the entire point.
  • 4Return slowly in 3 seconds. Only arms and shoulders move — zero torso rocking.
Trainer Tip: This corrects forward head posture from sitting all day. Better posture = you look slimmer the instant you stand up straight.
EX 3
Dumbbell Bicep Curl
Biceps · Brachialis
3Sets
12Reps
45sRest
  • 1Stand with dumbbells, arms extended at sides, palms facing forward.
  • 2Upper arms pinned to sides — they must NOT move during the curl at all.
  • 3Curl up, squeeze hard at the top for 1 second.
  • 4Lower SLOWLY in 3 seconds — the slow lowering builds more muscle than the curl itself.
Trainer Tip: Most beginners drop the weight fast on the way down — that's leaving 50% of gains on the table every single rep.
🚶
Finish: 15 Min Incline Treadmill Walk
Speed 5.5–6 km/h · Incline 8–10% · Consistency here is your secret weapon for steady fat loss.
💡
Why Back Training Visually Shrinks Your Belly

Weak back = shoulders roll forward = belly protrudes. 4 weeks of back training improves posture so dramatically that people think you've lost 5kg before the scale even moves.

03
LEG DAY
Quads · Hamstrings · Glutes · Calves — Biggest muscles = most calories burned per session
QUADSHAMSTRINGSGLUTES~55 MIN
EXERCISES
EX 1
Leg Press
Quads · Glutes · Hamstrings
3Sets
15Reps
75sRest
  • 1Sit on leg press, back flat. Feet hip-width apart in the center of the platform.
  • 2Release safety. Lower platform until knees reach 90°. Knees track over toes.
  • 3Press through HEELS back up. Don't fully lock out knees at the top.
  • 4Lower back stays pressed to seat throughout. Never let hips lift off.
Trainer Tip: Higher foot placement = glutes/hamstrings. Lower = quads. Leg day burns most calories of any session — never skip it.
EX 2
Goblet Squat
Quads · Glutes · Core — Best Beginner Squat
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
  • 1Hold dumbbell vertically at chest, both hands cupping top end. Feet wider than shoulder-width, toes out 30°.
  • 2Chest up, core braced. Push knees out over toes as you squat — aim for hips below knees.
  • 3Dumbbell counterbalances and keeps you upright. Elbows brush inner thighs at the bottom.
  • 4Drive through heels, squeeze glutes hard at top. Breathe out on the way up.
Trainer Tip: Getting low activates glutes 3× more than a shallow squat. Depth is everything here.
EX 3
Romanian Deadlift
Hamstrings · Glutes · Lower Back
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
LightWeight
  • 1Stand feet hip-width, dumbbells in front of thighs, palms facing you.
  • 2HINGE at hips — push butt back, NOT bend at waist. Flat back throughout. This is the key move to learn.
  • 3Lower dumbbells along your legs until you feel a deep hamstring stretch. Stop before back rounds.
  • 4Drive hips forward to stand, squeeze glutes hard at the top.
Trainer Tip: Very light weight — technique first. Getting the hinge right protects your lower back and builds incredible hamstrings over time.
🚴
Finish: 20 Min Moderate Cycling
RPM 70–80 · Medium resistance · Keeps blood moving, accelerates leg recovery, burns extra fat.
04
CORE BLAST + CARDIO
Your belly-attack day. Where the real transformation happens. No skipping — ever.
ABSCORECARDIO~55 MIN
CORE CIRCUIT — 3 ROUNDS · 30s REST BETWEEN ROUNDS
CORE 1
Plank Hold
Deep Core · Transverse Abdominis
3Rounds
30sHold
30sRest
  • 1Rest on forearms and toes. Elbows directly under shoulders. Perfectly straight line head to heels.
  • 2Squeeze glutes. Brace core as if someone is about to punch your stomach.
  • 3Hold 30 seconds breathing slowly. Build toward 60 seconds by Week 4.
  • 4If hips sag, take knees down. Perfect form beats longer duration every time.
Trainer Tip: The plank trains the transverse abdominis — your deepest ab muscle, your natural corset. This is what actually pulls your stomach IN. Most important core exercise you'll do.
CORE 2
Lying Leg Raises
Lower Abs · Hip Flexors
3Rounds
15Reps
30sRest
  • 1Lie flat on back, legs straight. Hands under glutes. Press lower back into floor throughout.
  • 2Raise both legs up to 90°, then lower slowly — stop just before feet touch the floor.
  • 3Take 3–4 seconds to lower. Slower = harder work on lower abs.
  • 4Too hard? Bend knees slightly. Straighten them as you get stronger week by week.
Trainer Tip: Lower abs are the last to show and last to go — these are exactly what create the flat belly look. Do them every Day 4 without fail.
CORE 3
Mountain Climbers
Core · Cardio · Hip Flexors
3Rounds
30sEach
30sRest
  • 1High plank — hands under shoulders, body in a straight line from head to heels.
  • 2Drive right knee toward chest as far as possible, quickly switch — left in, right out.
  • 3Keep hips level throughout — don't let them rise or dip as you alternate legs.
  • 4Move at a pace that elevates your heart rate. Core braced the entire time.
Trainer Tip: 2-in-1: core training AND cardio simultaneously. Burns belly fat from outside while building strength from inside. Pure gold for your goal.
🔥
Main Cardio: 25–30 Min Steady State
Treadmill / Elliptical / Cycling at 65–70% max heart rate. Slightly breathless but can still talk. After core circuit, your body taps directly into fat stores.
⚠️
The Belly Fat Truth

You cannot crunch away belly fat. Crunches build the muscle underneath — cardio and a calorie deficit burn the fat on top. Day 4 does both. Core first, then cardio. Non-negotiable.

EX 2
Arnold Press
All 3 Deltoid Heads — Full Shoulder
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
  • 1Sit with back support. Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing YOU — like top of a bicep curl.
  • 2As you press UP, rotate palms to face FORWARD. This rotation is the key move.
  • 3Press all the way up, arms extended, palms now facing away at the top.
  • 4Lower in reverse — rotate back so palms face you at the bottom. Full rotation every rep.
Trainer Tip: Hits all 3 parts of shoulder in one movement. Builds that rounded capped shoulder look creating the V-taper illusion.
EX 3
Cable Row (Close Grip)
Mid Back · Lats · Biceps
3Sets
12Reps
60sRest
  • 1Same setup as Day 2 — close-grip neutral handle if available.
  • 2Sit tall, pull to midsection, shoulder blade squeeze at the end of every rep.
  • 3Should feel more coordinated vs Day 2. That IS progress.
Trainer Tip: Each week the same exercise feels easier — that's your signal to increase the weight slightly. That's how you keep progressing.
